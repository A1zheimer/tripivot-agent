# Adobe Systems

Adobe, formerly known as Adobe Systems, was founded in December 1982 by John Warnock and Charles Gschke, both formerly working at the Palo Alto Research Centre of Schulling, and left and built Adobe, which enabled the PostScript page to describe the commercial use of the language. The company’s name, Adobe, is from Los Atus, California, and the river is not far from the company’s office in Mountain View, California.
